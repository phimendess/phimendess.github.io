Music · Prince and The Revolution

Purple Rain

Introduction

Purple Rain is a song by Prince and The Revolution. Released in 1984, it shares its name with an album and a film. The album also serves as the film's soundtrack.

Origins

Prince first developed the track as a country song and considered working with Stevie Nicks. He associated its imagery with love, faith and facing the end of the world.

Recording

The recording began with a live performance at First Avenue in Minneapolis on 3 August 1983. The concert raised funds for the Minnesota Dance Theatre.

Reception and legacy

Music publications including Rolling Stone, Q and Pitchfork have recognised the track in their rankings. It also appears in the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame's selection of influential songs.
